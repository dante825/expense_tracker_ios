# Privacy Policy — Expense Tracker

*Last updated: 3 May 2026*

## Overview

Expense Tracker does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data. All information you enter — expenses, income, categories, tags, and receipt photos — is stored exclusively on your device using Apple's CoreData framework.

## Data Collection

We do not collect any data. There are no analytics, no crash-reporting SDKs, no advertising networks, and no third-party services integrated into the app.

## Data Storage

All app data is stored locally on your device. It is included in your device's standard iCloud or iTunes backup if you have backups enabled — this is managed entirely by Apple and never accessible to us.

## Data Sharing

We do not share any data with third parties because we never receive any data in the first place.

## Children's Privacy

The app does not collect data from anyone, including children under 13.

## Changes to This Policy

If the app is updated in a way that changes data handling, this policy will be updated and the "Last updated" date above will be revised.

## Contact

If you have any questions about this privacy policy, please open an issue at the app's GitHub repository.
