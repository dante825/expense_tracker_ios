## Description (4000 chars max)
Take full control of your personal finances with a simple, private expense tracker. Every record stays on your device — no accounts, no cloud, no subscriptions.

**Track Expenses & Income**
Log transactions in seconds. Assign categories, mark entries as income or expense, and attach receipt photos directly to any record.

**Budget by Category**
Set spending limits per category and track your progress at a glance. See exactly where your money is going with a spending-by-category donut chart.

**Automate Recurring Bills**
Set up daily, weekly, or monthly recurring entries for rent, subscriptions, and regular bills. The app materialises them automatically on each due date.

**Dashboard at a Glance**
Filter by time period — Week, Month, Year, or All Time — and drill down with tag filters. See total income, total spent, net balance, average per entry, and savings rate in one view.

**Organise with Tags**
Add custom tags to any expense and filter your entire history by tag. Rename or remove tags at any time.

**Completely Private**
All data is stored on-device using Apple's CoreData framework. Nothing is sent to any server. No account required.
